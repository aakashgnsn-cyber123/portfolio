01 / About

About Me

I'm a BCA student at Mount Carmel College with a growing interest in how the web comes together — the way a good interface feels simple on the surface while a lot of logic runs quietly underneath it. That mix of design and reasoning is what first pulled me toward development, and it's what keeps me curious.

I'm naturally drawn to figuring out how things work rather than just using them, which shows up in how I learn: picking apart a layout, testing an idea in the console, or rebuilding a small feature just to understand it properly. Software feels like an area with an endless amount to explore, and I like that I'm still early in that journey.

Alongside my coursework, I'm strengthening my programming fundamentals and DSA, and building small hands-on projects to practice turning what I learn into working code.
